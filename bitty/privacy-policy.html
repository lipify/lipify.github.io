Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bitty: Baby Tracker & Diary ("Bitty", "the App", "we", "us") handles information. Bitty is made by Lipify.

  • Your diary is stored only on your phone. We have no servers and never receive it.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • You decide when to share an export or a doctor report, and with whom.

1. Information We Collect

We, the developer, do not collect, receive or sell any personal information. The App does not send your diary or any other data to us or to any third party. There is no sign-up and no account.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

To work, the App stores what you enter in a database on your device:

  • Child profiles: name (or nickname), date of birth and a colour.
  • Diary entries: feeding and drinks, diapers and toilet, sleep, medicines and medicine courses (name, dose, interval, daily limit), temperature readings, times and optional notes. Some of this is health information about your child.
  • Settings: for example night mode and reminder preferences.

This data never leaves your device unless you share it yourself (section 4) or your device backup is on (section 3). It is protected by your device's own security, such as the screen lock and storage encryption.

3. Device Backup

If backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the App's data in your device backup to your Google account and restore it when you set up a new phone or reinstall the App. This backup is handled by Android and Google under your Google account; we have no access to it. You can turn it off in your device settings (Settings → Google → Backup).

4. Exports and Doctor Reports

In Settings you can export your diary to a file, and you can create a PDF report for a doctor. These files are created on your device and shared only when you choose a destination in Android's share sheet (for example email, a messenger or cloud storage). After that, the app or service you chose handles the file under its own privacy policy. Importing a file replaces the data in the App with the data from that file.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications (Android 13 and later ask for permission), the App schedules local reminders on your device, for example when the next dose of a medicine course may be given. Reminders are created and shown by your device; nothing is sent over the internet. You can turn reminders off for each course or in your device settings.

6. Permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — medicine-dose reminders, see section 5.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) and vibration — to restore scheduled reminders after the phone restarts and to alert you.

The App does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos. When you import a backup, you pick the file yourself in Android's file picker; the App reads only that file.

7. Third-Party Services

The App contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

  • Google Play — app distribution and updates. Google Privacy Policy
  • Android backup — see section 3. Google Privacy Policy

Links in the App (this policy, the Terms of Use, contacting us by email) open in your browser or email app.

8. Children's Privacy

Bitty is a tool for parents and caregivers. It is intended for adults and is not directed at children. Information about a child is entered by the parent or caregiver and stays on their device; we do not receive it. If you believe a child has used the App, all data can be removed as described in section 9.

9. Your Rights and Data Deletion

  • Access and portability: all your data is in the App; you can export it at any time in Settings.
  • Correction and deletion: you can edit or delete any entry, medicine course or child profile in the App. Deleted items disappear from the App right away; they are erased from the device's storage completely when you clear the App's storage or uninstall it (see below).
  • Erase everything: uninstall the App or clear its storage (Settings → Apps → Bitty → Storage → Clear storage). This permanently removes all data from the device. To also remove it from your Google account backup, delete the App's backup in your device backup settings.

Because we do not hold any of your data, we cannot view, restore or delete it for you. If you live in the EU/EEA or the UK, you have rights under the GDPR; as the data never reaches us, you exercise them directly on your device as described above.

10. Medical Disclaimer

Bitty is a diary. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Doses and treatment are decided by your doctor. See the Terms of Use.

11. Changes to This Policy

If a future version of the App changes how data is handled (for example optional cloud sync), we will update this policy before that version is released. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
