Brainstage

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Brainstage ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. Brainstage is made by Lipify.

1. Information We Collect

We, the developer, do not collect or receive any personal information. We have no servers and no access to your data. Everything you create in the App is stored only on your device.

The App includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob), which collects certain data from your device to show ads, as described in section 3: your IP address (used to estimate approximate location), interactions with ads and the App, diagnostic information, and device identifiers such as the advertising ID. This data is sent to Google, encrypted in transit, and used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

  • Your game progress: coins, experience and level, best blitz score, daily ladder streak and today's result, trophies, correct answers per topic, and which questions you have already seen (so the App can show new ones).
  • Your avatar, title and studio choices, and items you unlocked in the shop.
  • Whether you bought Brainstage VIP (the purchase itself is also kept by Google Play, so it can be restored).
  • App settings and preferences: language and sound.
  • All quiz questions are built into the App; playing does not send your answers anywhere. Sharing your daily result only happens when you tap Share, and you choose where to send it.

If backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the App's local data in your device backup to your Google account and restore it when you reinstall the App. This backup is handled by Android and Google; we have no access to it.

3. Advertising

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Ads are limited to a family-friendly content rating. Google may use your advertising identifier to show personalized ads; you can reset it or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings.

Ad consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent with Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested, and personalized ads are shown only if you agree. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the App: Settings → Privacy options. If you decline, you may still see non-personalized or limited ads.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — advertising. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google User Messaging Platform — ad consent where required. About UMP
  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Google Play under your Google account. We do not receive your payment information. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play — app distribution and updates. The App may ask you to rate it using the Google Play In-App Review dialog; your rating and review go directly to Google Play under your Google account, and we never see who left them.

5. Permissions

The App asks you for no runtime permissions. It declares only the following install-time permissions, which are granted automatically:

  • Internet and network state — to load ads and to talk to Google Play for purchases. Questions and your progress never leave your device.
  • Advertising ID and ad services (added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK) — for ads as described in section 3.
  • Google Play Billing — for the optional Brainstage VIP purchase.
  • Wake lock and foreground service (added by Google libraries) — used internally by those libraries; the App does not run in the background.

6. Children's Privacy

Brainstage is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Your Rights and Data Deletion

  • Access and deletion: all data is on your device. You can delete it in the App or by uninstalling the App. To also remove it from your Google account backup, delete the App's backup in your device backup settings.
  • Ads: change your ad consent in Settings → Privacy options (where shown) and ad personalization in your device settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at lipify24@gmail.com.
