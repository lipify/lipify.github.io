Healthlytics

Headache, period & blood pressure diary

Healthlytics is a calm, private health diary for the things your doctor asks you to track: headaches and migraine, your menstrual cycle and blood pressure — plus anything else you want to follow, like weight, sleep or mood. Free, no account, no ads, works offline.

Get Healthlytics on Google Play →

What you can do

  • Headache and migraine diary — intensity, type, where it hurts, aura and symptoms, triggers and the medication you took, with whether it helped.
  • Period tracker — period days, flow and symptoms; a calendar forecast of your next period.
  • Blood pressure log — systolic, diastolic and pulse with a quick number pad; morning and evening averages.
  • Your own trackers — weight, blood sugar, sleep, mood, temperature, steps, water or anything else: a number, a 0–10 scale, yes/no or a note.
  • One calendar for everything, a searchable history and charts for 30 days, 90 days, a year or all time.
  • Patterns in your own records — for example headaches around the start of your period, by time of day or with a trigger — and a month summary.
  • PDF report for your doctor — a clear summary with calendars and tables to print or share.
  • Gentle reminders for blood pressure, an evening headache check-in, your cycle and your own trackers.

Private by design

  • No account, no ads, no analytics, and no internet permission.
  • Your records stay on your phone. Export everything as CSV at any time and import it on a new phone.
  • On a locked screen, reminders hide their text.

Questions

Is Healthlytics a medical app?

No. It is a diary, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat anything, and the cycle forecast is not a method of contraception. It helps you keep accurate notes to discuss with your doctor.

How do I move my diary to a new phone?

On the old phone, open Settings → Export CSV and save the ZIP file (for example to your cloud drive). On the new phone, open Settings → Import from file and choose that ZIP.

Which languages are supported?

English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Russian and Turkish.

Does it cost anything?

Healthlytics is free and has no ads.
