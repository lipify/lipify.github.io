Healthlytics

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Healthlytics is an offline health diary. Everything you record stays on your phone. We, the developer, have no servers and never receive your data. There are no ads, no analytics and no accounts.

This Privacy Policy explains how Healthlytics ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. Healthlytics is made by Lipify.

1. Information We Collect

We, the developer, do not collect, receive or sell any personal information. The App has no internet permission, no account, no analytics and no advertising SDK. Nothing you record is sent to us or to anyone else.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following only in its private storage on your device:

  • Headache records: start and end time, intensity, type, location, aura and symptoms, triggers, medications you took (name, dose, time, whether it helped) and notes.
  • Menstrual cycle records: period days, flow, symptoms and notes, and your usual cycle and period length. The forecast of your next period is calculated on your device.
  • Blood pressure records: systolic and diastolic values, pulse, time, arm, body position and notes.
  • Your own trackers (for example weight, sleep or mood) and their records.
  • Reminders you set up, and app settings (theme, first day of the week).

No backup. The App turns off Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer for its data. If you uninstall the App or change phones, your records are lost unless you export them first (Settings → Export CSV).

3. Exporting, Importing and Reports

  • Export: Settings → Export CSV creates a ZIP file with your records (CSV files and a data file for import) and opens the system share sheet.
  • Import: Settings → Import from file reads a ZIP you choose (for example the export from your old phone) and replaces the data in the App. The App only reads the file you pick; it does not access other files.
  • Report for the doctor: the App can create a PDF report of your records on your device. You decide whether to print it or share it.

Where an exported file or report goes (for example a cloud drive, email or another app) is your choice; from that moment it is handled by the service you selected, not by us.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play — app distribution and updates. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play In-App Review — the App may occasionally ask you to rate it using Google Play's own review dialog. Your rating and review go directly to Google Play under your Google account; we only see them as public reviews in Google Play.

5. Permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — only for the reminders you turn on (for example a blood pressure reminder, or the monthly summary, which is switched on together with your first reminder). The App asks for it the first time you turn a reminder on. Cycle reminders never mention your cycle. Reminders are marked as private: if your phone hides sensitive notification content on the lock screen, only “Healthlytics” is shown there.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE) — so that a reminder can vibrate when it arrives.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) — to restore your reminders after the phone restarts or the App is updated.

The App requests no other permissions: no internet, location, camera, contacts or storage access.

6. Health Information

Healthlytics is a personal diary, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and the cycle forecast is not a method of contraception. Your health records never leave your device unless you export them yourself.

7. Children's Privacy

Healthlytics is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions, please contact us.

8. Your Rights and Data Deletion

  • Access: all your data is in the App and can be exported at any time (Settings → Export CSV).
  • Deletion: delete single records in the App, delete everything with Settings → Delete all data, or uninstall the App. Because there is no backup, deleted data cannot be restored.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the App gets new features. Changes are posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
