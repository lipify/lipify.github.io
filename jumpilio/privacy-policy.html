Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Jumpilio ("the Game", "we", "us") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store, or receive any personal information. All game data (scores, achievements, settings, coins) is stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences and is never sent to our servers (we have none).

The Game includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), which collects some data from your device to show ads, as described in section 2.

2. Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display ads in the Game. The Game shows two types of ads:

  • Interstitial ads — full-screen ads shown periodically between games
  • Rewarded video ads — optional ads you can watch to earn in-game rewards (coin multipliers, revives)

All ads are limited to content rated PG or below.

AdMob may collect data to serve ads, including:

  • Advertising ID (Android Ad ID / IDFA)
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • General location (IP-based, not precise GPS)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and Google Ads Policy. We do not have access to this data.

Ad consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), a consent tool certified under the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework. Until you make a choice, ads are not requested. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the Game: Settings → Privacy options. If you decline, you may still see non-personalized or limited ads.

3. Third-Party Services

The Game uses the following third-party services that may collect data:

  • Google AdMob — advertising (AdMob Privacy)
  • Google User Messaging Platform — ad consent form (UMP)
  • Google Play Services — app distribution and updates

4. Children's Privacy

Jumpilio is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The Game:

  • Does not send any personal data to us
  • Restricts all ads to content rated PG or below
  • Contains no user accounts, social features, or chat functionality

If you are a parent or guardian and have any concerns, please contact us.

5. Data Storage and Security

All game progress (scores, achievements, statistics, coins, settings, purchased skins) is stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences. We do not operate any servers or databases that store user data. If Android backup is turned on for your device, this local game data may be included in your device backup to your Google account and restored when you reinstall the Game; this backup is handled by Android and Google, and we have no access to it. Uninstalling the Game deletes the data from your device; to also remove it from backups, delete the Game's backup in your Google account settings (Google → Backup).

The Game does not require an internet connection to play. Internet is only used to load and display advertisements.

6. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data for us to provide, modify, or delete. You can change your ad consent in the Game (Settings → Privacy options, where available), and reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalization

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the Game after changes constitutes acceptance.
