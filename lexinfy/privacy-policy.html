Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Welcome to Lexinfy ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our word-search and vocabulary-learning game. Lexinfy is designed for a general audience, and its content and ads are family-friendly.

1. Information We Collect

We, the developer, do not collect or receive any personal information. We have no servers and no access to your data.

All game data — including your progress, learned words, coins, streak, settings, and preferences — is stored exclusively on your device using local storage and is not sent to us.

The App includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob), which collects certain data from your device to show ads, as described in section 3: your IP address (used to estimate approximate location), interactions with ads and the App, diagnostic information, and device identifiers such as the advertising ID. This data is sent to Google, encrypted in transit, and used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally on your device to provide its functionality:

  • Game progress: learned words, coins earned, daily streak, achievements, and saved game state.
  • Preferences: language, theme (light/dark), sound and text-to-speech settings, difficulty level.
  • Learning data: flashcard review queue, word translations, and daily challenge progress.
  • Reminder settings: whether daily reminders are on and the time you chose.

You can delete all locally stored data at any time through the App's settings ("Reset Progress") or by uninstalling the App.

3. Advertising

The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob:

  • Ads are limited to a family-friendly content rating (PG).
  • Interstitial ads are shown no more than once every 3 games, with at least 1 minute between ads.
  • Rewarded ads are optional and user-initiated (e.g., to earn hints).
  • Google AdMob may use your advertising identifier to serve personalized ads. You can opt out of ad personalization in your device settings.

Ad consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested, and personalized ads are shown only if you agree. To change your choice later, clear the App's data in Android settings (Settings → Apps → Lexinfy → Storage → Clear storage); the consent form will be shown again the next time you open the App. Note that this also resets your progress.

Google AdMob may collect certain device information (such as advertising identifier, device type, and OS version) to serve ads. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google User Messaging Platform — for asking for ad consent where required. About UMP
  • Google Play In-App Review — the App may ask you to rate it using Google Play's review dialog. Your review is submitted to Google Play under your Google account; we do not see whether the dialog was shown or what you rated.
  • Text-to-Speech — uses your device's built-in TTS engine for word pronunciation. No audio data is transmitted externally.

When you share the App or a game result, your device's share menu opens and you choose where to send it. We do not receive what you share.

5. Notifications

Daily reminders are off by default. If you turn them on in Settings, the App asks for permission to show notifications and schedules reminders on your device at the time you choose. Reminders are created locally and are restored after your device restarts (this is why the App may start briefly when your device boots). No reminder data leaves your device. You can turn reminders off in the App's settings or in your device's notification settings at any time.

6. Internet Access

The App requires internet access solely for loading advertisements. All game functionality works offline.

7. Data Security

Since all user data is stored locally on your device and we do not collect or transmit any personal information, your data is protected by your device's built-in security features.

If backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the App's local data in your device backup to your Google account and restore it when you reinstall the App. This backup is handled by Android and Google; we have no access to it.

8. Children's Privacy

Lexinfy is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the App are limited to a family-friendly content rating (PG). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Access & Deletion: All data is on your device. You can reset your progress in settings or uninstall the App to delete all data. To also remove it from your Google account backup, delete the App's backup in your device backup settings.
  • Opt out of ads: You can adjust ad personalization settings in your device's system settings, and change your ad consent as described in section 3.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
