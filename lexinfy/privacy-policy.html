Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 12, 2026

Welcome to Lexinfy ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our word-search and vocabulary-learning game. Lexinfy is designed for a general audience and suitable for the whole family.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

All game data — including your progress, learned words, coins, streak, settings, and preferences — is stored exclusively on your device using local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally on your device to provide its functionality:

  • Game progress: learned words, coins earned, daily streak, achievements, and saved game state.
  • Preferences: language, theme (light/dark), sound and text-to-speech settings, difficulty level.
  • Learning data: flashcard review queue, word translations, and daily challenge progress.

You can delete all locally stored data at any time through the App's settings ("Reset Progress") or by uninstalling the App.

3. Advertising

The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob:

  • Ads are limited to a family-friendly content rating (PG).
  • Interstitial ads are shown no more than once every 3 games, with at least 1 minute between ads.
  • Rewarded ads are optional and user-initiated (e.g., to earn hints).
  • Google AdMob may use your advertising identifier to serve personalized ads. You can opt out of ad personalization in your device settings.

Google AdMob may collect certain device information (such as advertising identifier, device type, and OS version) to serve ads. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements. Google Privacy Policy
  • Text-to-Speech — uses your device's built-in TTS engine for word pronunciation. No audio data is transmitted externally.

5. Internet Access

The App requires internet access solely for loading advertisements. All game functionality works offline.

6. Data Security

Since all user data is stored locally on your device and we do not collect or transmit any personal information, your data is protected by your device's built-in security features.

7. Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Access & Deletion: All data is on your device. You can reset your progress in settings or uninstall the App to delete all data.
  • Opt out of ads: You can adjust ad personalization settings in your device's system settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
