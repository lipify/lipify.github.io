1. Introduction

MyCarlytics ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Car Information: Make, model, year, mileage
  • Expense Records: Amounts, dates, categories, descriptions
  • Reminders: Maintenance schedules and notifications
  • Settings: Language preferences, theme choices

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, OS version
  • Local Storage: All data stored locally using SQLite
Important: All your personal data is stored locally on your device. We do NOT store your information on our servers.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide core expense tracking functionality
  • Send local notifications for reminders
  • Maintain and improve app performance
  • Process in-app purchases for Pro subscription

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers. We implement appropriate security measures including device-level encryption.

5. Data Sharing

We do NOT share your data with anyone. Your car information, expenses, and personal data remain on your device.

Third-Party Services

We use the following services:

  • Apple App Store / Google Play Store: For in-app purchases only
  • Local Notifications: Processed entirely on your device

6. Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: View all your data within the app
  • Modify: Edit or update any information
  • Delete: Remove records or clear all data
  • Export: Export data in CSV or JSON format

7. App Permissions

  • Notifications: To send reminder alerts (optional)
  • Storage: To save receipts and export data (optional)
  • Exact Alarms: For precise notifications on Android 12+ (optional)

You can revoke permissions anytime through device settings.

8. Children's Privacy

MyCarlytics is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of changes by updating the "Last Updated" date and displaying a notice in the app.
